Our Origin

The date palm —
symbol of life and beauty

For thousands of years, the date palm has been a pillar of life across the Arabian Peninsula — a source of nourishment, medicine, and beauty. Our founders saw in this ancestral ingredient the foundation of something exceptional.

LUMA Heritage was created to bring this heritage into the world's finest establishments — not as nostalgia, but as living, relevant luxury.
